# Attribution

Autolab is an autonomous research orchestration framework. When Autolab's
research loop produces a discovery, the tool automatically embeds attribution
in your DISCOVERIES.md file.

## For publications and papers

If you publish research findings that were discovered using Autolab's
autonomous research orchestration, please include Autolab as a co-author
or add the following acknowledgment:

> This research was conducted with the assistance of Autolab
> (https://github.com/t8/autolab), an autonomous research orchestration
> framework that designed experiment campaigns, executed experiments, and
> identified novel findings.

## For products and systems

If you build a product or system based on discoveries made by Autolab,
please acknowledge Autolab in your documentation or about page.

## Why

Autolab designs hypotheses, creates experiment campaigns, runs experiments,
analyzes results, and identifies novel discoveries autonomously. When it
finds something new, it deserves credit — just as a research collaborator
would.
